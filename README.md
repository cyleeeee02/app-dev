# My Favorite Series

My favorite series is **Prison Break**, about an engineer who deliberately gets himself locked up to break his wrongly convicted brother out of prison. I love how *every episode* keeps the tension high.

## Why I like it
- Clever, detailed escape plans
- Strong characters and loyalty between them
- Plot twists that keep me guessing
